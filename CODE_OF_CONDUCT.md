# Code of Conduct

## Community standards

Contributors and maintainers are expected to collaborate respectfully and constructively. Be welcoming to participants regardless of background or experience. Discuss code and ideas rather than attacking people.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning language, sustained disruption, unwanted disclosure of personal information, and sharing another person's private communications without permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

Raise routine project concerns in an issue or pull request. For harassment, privacy concerns, or other sensitive conduct, contact the repository maintainers through a private channel listed on their GitHub profiles rather than posting identifiable details publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, issue warnings, limit participation, or ban accounts from project spaces. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and based on the reported conduct.
